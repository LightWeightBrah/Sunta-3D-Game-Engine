# Sounds and music of the sample game

All sound effects are from [Freesound](https://freesound.org) and are **CC0** (public domain): free to use, change and share, no credit required. They are listed anyway, so it is clear where every sound comes from.
They were found and downloaded with `Tools/Audio/freesound_client.py` (only CC0 is searched, every download is written down in `Tools/Audio/freesound_log.json`), chosen by ear on an audition page, and turned into Ogg Vorbis with `Tools/Audio/to_ogg.py`: mono for sounds heard from a point in the world, the silence at the start cut off, every sound brought to a loudness that fits its role (footsteps quieter than blows, the roar louder).
