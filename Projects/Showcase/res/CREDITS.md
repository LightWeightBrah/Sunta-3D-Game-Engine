# Showcase project

Small scenes that show the engine's features (the pictures of the README and the docs), made by
`Projects/Showcase/Tools/build_showcase.py`. The characters are the engine's own (`Sunta/res/Models`).

`Skyboxes/BurningDusk`: [The Sky Is On Fire](https://polyhaven.com/a/the_sky_is_on_fire) from Poly Haven (CC0), turned
into a cubemap and tinted by `Tools/Skybox/panorama_to_cubemap.py` (the same sky as Strongest in History Vs Today).
